Can AI fill the shop filters your catalogue is missing?

Most products in a large furniture catalogue have no style, color, shape or material value, so they never show up when a shopper uses those filters. An n8n workflow asked Claude to read each product's text and fill the gaps, with a quote as evidence. Here is how often it was right, compared with a simple keyword matcher, on 259 real products whose values were hidden and then checked.

Scorecard by attribute

"Right when filled" is how often a filled value matches the catalogue's own value. "Gaps filled" is how many of the hidden values the method filled at all. Both methods read the same text: product name, category and the first 1,200 characters of the description.
